Cancellation and Refund Policy

Customers may request cancellation before the restaurant starts preparing the order.

Once preparation has started, cancellation may not be possible because ingredients and preparation resources may already have been committed.

If an order is cancelled successfully before preparation, the applicable refund is initiated to the original payment method.

For a payment-deducted but failed-order issue, support review is required and the issue should be escalated through a support ticket.
